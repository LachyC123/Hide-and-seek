Play safe

    

      
• Play only in safe, public, permitted places.

      
• Never trespass. Never play near traffic you can't cross safely.

      
• Obey roads and crossings. Look up, not down.

      
• Never play while driving.

      
• Never enter somewhere dangerous just to stay in the zone. Being exposed is better than being hurt.

      
• The host picks the play area and is responsible for it.
